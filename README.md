# Ember

A primer on **life as chemistry and heat**. Eight chapters and four labs on metabolism viewed from thermodynamics: open systems, the second law, gradients, redox, ATP, membranes, glycolysis, and respiration.

Life is not a bag of energetic molecules. It is an unfinished flow. Equilibrium is death.

## Path

1. **Equilibrium is death** — a living cell is an open system
2. **The second law is not our enemy** — local order, paid for in heat
3. **Gradients are the currency** — work requires a difference
4. **Food is reduced carbon** — we eat electrons and dump them on oxygen
5. **ATP is not a battery** — the ratio is the treasure
6. **The membrane as a dam** — chemiosmosis
7. **Life before the waterfall** — glycolysis
8. **The roundabout and the waterfall** — the citric acid cycle and the ETC

## Labs

- Closed versus open — watch order collapse, then hold it with a flux
- The redox tower — drop electrons from a donor to an acceptor
- The proton dam — pump, hold, and spend a membrane gradient
- ATP’s real ΔG — slide the mass-action ratio

Progress is stored in the browser (`localStorage`). No account required.

## Run locally
